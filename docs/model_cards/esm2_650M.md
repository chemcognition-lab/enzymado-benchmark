## 0. How to think about this model

**ESM-2 650M** (`facebook/esm2_t33_650M_UR50D`, Enzymado key `esm2_650M`) is a 33-layer, 1,280-dimensional bidirectional Transformer encoder (~651M parameters) trained purely on single 1D amino-acid sequences via 15% masked language modeling (MLM) over ~43M UniRef50 clusters (sampling uniformly across ~138M UniRef90 cluster members). Because it receives no explicit structural coordinates, multiple sequence alignments (MSAs), or 3Di structural tokens during training, 3D tertiary contact maps and atomic-level structural projections emerge implicitly inside its self-attention maps and final hidden states as a byproduct of co-evolutionary residue recovery. Within the ESM-2 scaling family (8M to 15B), the 650M checkpoint sits at the inflection point where sequences with high evolutionary depth largely saturate in unsupervised contact precision and structure-prediction TM-score, while maintaining a moderate per-residue embedding width ($D = 1{,}280$) that keeps downstream probe parameter counts compact relative to `esm2_3B` ($D = 2{,}560$).

- **Best-suited dataset regimes:**
  - Broad multi-family or enzyme-superfamily property datasets populated by natural UniRef-like homologs with moderate-to-high evolutionary depth.
  - Single- and multi-point mutational scanning or stability delta tasks ($\mathbf{e}_{\text{mut}} - \mathbf{e}_{\text{wt}}$) on natural protein scaffolds where local evolutionary site preferences and pairwise epistatic couplings govern fitness.
  - Per-residue active-site or binding-site annotation and learned attention-pooling regimes where a compact $D = 1{,}280$ residue embedding lowers over-parameterization risk relative to $D = 2{,}560$.
- **Expected vulnerabilities & blind spots:**
  - Orphan, metagenomic-singleton, or low-evolutionary-depth sequences where the language model exhibits high perplexity ($> 16$) and weaker emergent structural contacts than the 3B or 15B checkpoints.
  - *De novo* designed proteins (sequences annotated with `"de novo"` or `"artificial sequence"` were explicitly filtered out of the UniRef pretraining corpus) and non-natural chemical modifications.
  - Proteins substantially longer than the 1,024-token pretraining crop window, which rely on Rotary Position Embedding (RoPE) extrapolation at extraction time.
- **Downstream probe interaction summary:** Pretrained with zero hidden and attention dropout (`dropout = 0.0`) to maximize sequence-modeling capacity, producing dense 1,280-dimensional residue representations. Mean-pooled linear, MLP, and XGBoost probes operate on a compact 1,280-D vector, while per-residue attention pooling (`attention_linear`, `attention_mlp`) fits half as many attention parameters per token as on `esm2_3B` ($D = 2{,}560$).

## 1. Representational mechanisms & dataset compatibility guide

### 1.1 Objective & latent geometry
- **Mechanism:** Trained exclusively with a 15% masked language modeling (MLM) objective over single sequences (80% `<mask>`, 10% random amino acid, 10% unchanged), ESM-2 must infer evolutionary constraints and 3D spatial proximity from co-varying residue distributions across UniRef. Consequently, residue–residue contact maps emerge in the self-attention weights and atomic-level 3D coordinates become projectable from the internal representations without structural supervision, with validation perplexity correlating almost perfectly with structure-projection TM-score across scales ($r = -0.99$ on CASP14, $r = -1.00$ on CAMEO).
  > *"Transformer models that are trained with masked language modeling are known to develop attention patterns that correspond to the residue–residue contact map of the protein"* `[Main, p. 3]`
  > *"there is a correlation of −0.99 between validation perplexity and CASP14 TM-score and −1.00 between validation perplexity and CAMEO TM-score"* `[Main, p. 4]`
- **Downstream task implications:** Mean-pooled and per-residue representations naturally encode both local mutational tolerance (site-wise evolutionary log-odds) and global co-evolutionary fold identity, making them well-suited for mutational delta tasks ($\mathbf{e}_{\text{mut}} - \mathbf{e}_{\text{wt}}$), active-site residue classification, and sequence-to-function regression across natural homologs.

### 1.2 Pretraining corpus filtering & sampling
- **Mechanism:** Pretrained on UniRef50 (September 2021 version, ~43M training clusters) by sampling a UniRef50 cluster and replacing it with a sequence sampled uniformly from the corresponding UniRef90 cluster (~138M sequences total; ~65M unique sequences seen over 500K updates), while explicitly removing artificial and *de novo* designed proteins via taxonomy and Jackhmmer filters (58,462 proteins removed). At the 650M scale, the authors demonstrate that sequences with high evolutionary depth saturate in structural accuracy, whereas low-evolutionary-depth sequences require scaling to 3B–15B.
  > *"sequences are sampled with even weighting across ∼43 million UniRef50 training clusters from ∼138 million UniRef90 sequences"* `[Main, p. 2]`
  > *"Each sequence is then replaced with a sequence sampled uniformly from the corresponding UniRef90 cluster."* `[SI, p. 2]`
  > *"De-novo designed proteins are filtered out from the pretraining dataset via two filters."* `[SI, p. 2]`
  > *"Improvement on sequences with high evolutionary depth thus saturates at lower model scales"* `[Main, p. 3]`
- **Downstream task implications:** Provides broad coverage across natural bacterial, archaeal, and eukaryotic enzyme families represented in UniProt, with uniform UniRef50 cluster weighting preventing abundant housekeeping families from dominating the representation. However, synthetic/*de novo* scaffolds and rare orphan sequences with shallow evolutionary depth remain out-of-distribution or under-resolved relative to larger checkpoints.

### 1.3 Training crop window & positional encoding
- **Mechanism:** Replaces ESM-1b's learned absolute positional embeddings with Rotary Position Embedding (RoPE) applied to query and key vectors, and trains on random 1,024-token crops of longer sequences with `<cls>` (BOS) and `<eos>` (EOS) boundary tokens distinguishing full-length proteins from cropped fragments. In 150M-parameter ablations (Table S5), RoPE improves unsupervised long-range contact precision (`LR P@L = 0.381` vs. `0.365` without RoPE) and permits length extrapolation beyond the training crop window.
  > *"In ESM-2, we used Rotary Position Embedding (RoPE) (66) to allow the model to extrapolate beyond the context window it is trained on."* `[SI, p. 4]`
  > *"we cropped long proteins to random 1024 tokens. We used BOS and EOS tokens to signal the beginning and end of a real protein, to allow the model to separate a full-sized protein from a cropped one."* `[SI, p. 4]`
  > *"No RoPE 0.365 0.599 8.62"* `[SI, p. 21, Table S5]`
- **Downstream task implications:** Enzymado extracts full-length sequences without explicit truncation; single- and multi-domain enzymes $\le 1{,}024$ residues lie strictly inside the pretraining crop window, while longer enzymes ($> 1{,}024$ aa) rely on RoPE sinusoidal extrapolation across all 33 self-attention layers.

### 1.4 Embedding width $D$, pretraining dropout & downstream probe parameterization
- **Mechanism:** ESM-2 completely removes hidden-layer and attention dropout during pretraining (`dropout = 0.0`) to free up model capacity. At 650M parameters, the final layer outputs $D = 1{,}280$ features per residue—half the width of `esm2_3B` ($D = 2{,}560$) and roughly $1.25\times$ the width of `prost5` ($D = 1{,}024$).
  > *"ESM-1b had dropout both in hidden layers and attention which we removed completely to free up more capacity."* `[SI, p. 4]`
  > *"Embedding dim 320 480 640 1280 2560 5120"* `[SI, p. 19, Table S3]`
- **Downstream task implications:** The moderate $D = 1{,}280$ width keeps parameter counts balanced across both mean-pooled probes (`linear`, `mlp`, `xgboost`) and learned per-residue attention pooling (`attention_linear`, `attention_mlp` over $L \times 1{,}280$), reducing the risk of overfitting unregularized token states when downstream training sets are small ($N < 500$) or experimental assay noise is high.

## 4. Paper-Reported Limitations, Biases & Caveats

1. **Degradation on high-perplexity / poorly understood sequences:** Structural projection quality drops sharply when the language model assigns high perplexity to an input sequence (e.g., sequences lacking evolutionary relatives in UniRef).
   > *"The perplexity of the unsuccessful sequence is 16.6, meaning the language model does not understand the input sequence."* `[Main, p. 3]`
2. **Exclusion of *de novo* designed proteins:** Artificial and *de novo* designed proteins were explicitly removed from the pretraining corpus (58,462 sequences via UniProt taxonomy search and Jackhmmer against 154 *de novo* PDB entries), making synthetic scaffolds out-of-distribution.
   > *"De-novo designed proteins are filtered out from the pretraining dataset via two filters."* `[SI, p. 2]`
3. **Unbenchmarked behavior beyond the 1,024-token crop window:** Although RoPE mathematically permits length extrapolation, pretraining cropped all sequences at 1,024 tokens and the paper does not benchmark embedding quality on sequences $> 1{,}024$ residues.
   > *"we cropped long proteins to random 1024 tokens."* `[SI, p. 4]`
4. **Nomenclature & unreported hyperparameter gaps in the SI:** SI Section A.2.4 refers to the 650M and 3B models as `"ESM2 700M"` and `"2.8B"`, while Table S3 uses `650M` and `3B`. Additionally, language-model training numeric precision (FP16/BF16), feed-forward hidden dimension ($d_{\text{ffn}}$), and activation function are not stated in the paper or SI.
   > *"ESM2 700M took 8 days to train."* `[SI, p. 4]`

## 5. Enzymado Embedding & Probing Specification

*(Status: `code` — deterministic pipeline from `SUPPORTING_CONTEXT_MODELS.md` and `embedding_scripts/esm2_embedding_script.py`)*

- **Enzymado Key & Checkpoint:** `esm2_650M` $\rightarrow$ Hugging Face `facebook/esm2_t33_650M_UR50D` (`651M` registry parameters, $D = 1{,}280$).
- **Model Loader:** `transformers.EsmModel.from_pretrained("facebook/esm2_t33_650M_UR50D").eval()` and `EsmTokenizer.from_pretrained("facebook/esm2_t33_650M_UR50D")`.
- **Non-Standard Amino Acid Sanitization:** Rare/ambiguous residues `U`, `Z`, and `O` are mapped to unknown `X` before tokenization (`seq.replace("U", "X").replace("Z", "X").replace("O", "X")`).
- **Tokenization & Special-Token Slicing:** Tokenized via `tokenizer(seqs, add_special_tokens=True, padding="longest", return_tensors="pt")` without explicit truncation. ESM-2 prepends `<cls>` at index `0` and appends `<eos>` at index `s_len + 1`; Enzymado slices `emb = outputs.last_hidden_state[batch_idx, 1 : s_len + 1]` from the final (33rd) encoder layer so that strictly the $L$ amino-acid residue representations $\mathbf{E} \in \mathbb{R}^{L \times 1280}$ are retained.
- **Storage Format:** Saved in `bfloat16` (`BF16`) safetensors shards both as per-residue matrices $\mathbf{E} \in \mathbb{R}^{L \times 1280}$ (`SafetensorsShardWriter`) and as mean-pooled sequence vectors $\mathbf{e}_{\text{prot}} = \frac{1}{L}\sum_{i=1}^L \mathbf{E}_i \in \mathbb{R}^{1280}$ (`SafetensorsPooledWriter`).
- **Downstream Probing Heads Evaluated (5 heads across 140 runs per PLM):**
  1. `linear` (`pooling="no_attention"`, `head="linear"`): Ridge / L2-regularized linear or logistic/SGD probe over mean-pooled $\mathbf{e}_{\text{prot}} \in \mathbb{R}^{1280}$.
  2. `mlp` (`pooling="no_attention"`, `head="mlp"`): 2-layer feed-forward neural network with normalization, activation, and dropout over mean-pooled $\mathbf{e}_{\text{prot}} \in \mathbb{R}^{1280}$.
  3. `xgboost` (`pooling="no_attention"`, `head="xgboost"`): Gradient-boosted decision trees (`XGBRegressor` / `XGBClassifier`) over mean-pooled $\mathbf{e}_{\text{prot}} \in \mathbb{R}^{1280}$.
  4. `attention_linear` (`pooling="attention"`, `head="linear"`): Learned per-residue attention pooling (`ResidualAttentionAggregation` for global tasks; `AttentionResidueMixer` for residue tasks) trained jointly with a linear readout head over $\mathbf{E} \in \mathbb{R}^{L \times 1280}$.
  5. `attention_mlp` (`pooling="attention"`, `head="mlp"`): Learned per-residue attention pooling trained jointly with a 2-layer MLP readout head over $\mathbf{E} \in \mathbb{R}^{L \times 1280}$.
- **Task-Layout Conditioning:**
  - **Layout A (`protein_global`):** Uses $\mathbf{e}_{\text{prot}} \in \mathbb{R}^{1280}$ (concatenated with standardized reaction conditions such as temperature, pH, and buffer one-hot indicators when present).
  - **Layout B (`protein_ligand_global`):** Concatenates $\mathbf{e}_{\text{prot}} \in \mathbb{R}^{1280}$ with 200 normalized RDKit 2D substrate descriptors (`descriptastorus` `RDKit2DNormalized`).
  - **Layout C (`mutation_delta_global`):** Uses the wild-type subtraction vector $\mathbf{e}_{\text{mut}} - \mathbf{e}_{\text{wt}} \in \mathbb{R}^{1280}$ for point-mutation thermostability ($\Delta T_m$, $\Delta\Delta G$) tasks.
  - **Layout D (`protein_residue`):** Predicts per-residue binary labels $\{0, 1\}^L$ directly from $\mathbf{E} \in \mathbb{R}^{L \times 1280}$.
